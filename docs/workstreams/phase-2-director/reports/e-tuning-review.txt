=== Dennis's turns before the morning handover (158 of 181 quiet-day breaches in the (d) year run) ===
Reproduced with the director off: Kamala in Raj's room, seeds 1-8, 3 weeks: 36 breaches, almost all Dennis's turn at
06:50-06:53 (without Kamala: 4). Cause (seed 1, Wed): Kamala wakes 06:00; her morning care is female-only and the night
carer is a man, so it waits. The floating carer (a woman) arrives 06:35 for Dennis's 06:50 two-person turn, and at 06:36
takes Kamala's 20-minute morning care instead (it has waited 36 minutes and scores higher); the turn starts 06:57.
The existing rule "a pressing reserved turn's only possible partner keeps to short work" didn't apply: nobody had
reserved the turn, because both people who could do it were free at once.
Fix (general, not a special case): the only people free for a pressing turn nobody has reserved yet keep to short
work or pressing turns (turn_team). First version held them for one turn only and missed Raj's night turns (calm 25);
letting them take any pressing turn fixed that. Result: calm 2 -> 1, with Kamala 15 -> 0, falls unchanged.

Also found: two carers started day breaks at 11:57 (seed 1, falls at 11:00) counting the floating carer and the
main-building carer as floor cover; both left at 11:58 and the floor was empty (a hard floor_cover violation, exposed
when a tuning rule's removal shifted the timing). Fix (general): a day break, a resumed break or going home counts only
staff on a shift as cover, not helpers who leave when their job is done.
And: an agency worker who fell ill off the wing was forgotten before recovering, so the recovery wasn't logged (the
outbreak still ended correctly); an ill agency worker is now kept until recovered.
